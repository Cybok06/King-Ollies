This notice explains how King Ollies handles information connected with accounts, purchases, applications, customer stores and support. It covers data bundles, checkers and forms, NSS and SHS assistance, business registration, passport and birth certificate assistance, affidavits, gazettes and attestation services. King Ollies is the service name used on this website. Contact our team using the details below for privacy enquiries or information about the entity responsible for a particular service.

1. Information you provide

Account information includes your name, username, email, phone and WhatsApp numbers, business name, optional referral code and password credentials. Order records can contain beneficiary phone numbers, selected services, amounts, order references, payment status and delivery information. Customer stores can contain branding, publicly displayed contact details, service selections, selling prices and payout details.

Application forms vary by service. They may request full and previous names, date and place of birth, gender, nationality, citizenship information, occupation, education history, examination or index numbers, grades, placement details, NSS numbers, preferred institutions or companies, district and region, residential and postal addresses, landmarks, GPS or digital addresses, telephone numbers and email addresses.

Document-related services may require Ghana Card or other identification numbers, TIN details, passport-related information, business activities, signatures, supporting records and information about parents, grandparents, guarantors or witnesses. We also receive application messages, enquiries, correction requests, complaints and any relevant information you choose to send to support. Provide only what is necessary for your chosen service; do not send unrelated identity records.

2. Sensitive records and information about other people

Identity numbers, dates of birth, family details, signatures and supporting documents can expose you or others to identity misuse if disclosed. Some applications may also reveal information subject to additional legal protections, such as health, religious beliefs or alleged offences. Not every identity detail has the same legal classification, but all require careful handling. Do not volunteer such additional information unless it is necessary and the team has explained why it is required.

If you submit information about a child, parent, witness, guarantor or another applicant, tell that person how their information will be used and establish that you have lawful authority to provide it. Do not impersonate anyone or submit documents obtained without permission. A parent or lawful guardian should handle a child's application where appropriate. Contact us before providing information if you are unsure of your authority.

3. Payments and payment evidence

Paystack processes online payments. We receive payment references, amounts, currency, status and verification information, which can include payer contact and transaction details. Wallet records show credits, debits and purchases. A configured One Time Sign Up fee is recorded separately from wallet deposits. Manual payments may involve MoMo transfer details and an uploaded proof image for admin review.

Do not send your card PIN, MoMo PIN, password, one-time code or full card credentials in application messages or proof images. Payment credentials should be entered only in the payment provider's interface. Crop or redact unrelated balances and transactions from a receipt while leaving the information required to verify your payment readable.

4. Technical information and browser storage

The app uses session cookies to maintain sign-in and access permissions. The browser may store cart contents, payment references and interface preferences in local storage. Login and operational records may include IP address, browser or device information, timestamps and security events. Clearing browser storage can remove saved carts or interrupt access to a guest receipt. Log out on shared devices and keep a copy of your receipt reference.

5. Purposes and grounds for processing

We use relevant information to create and secure accounts, fulfil requests, prepare applications, communicate with applicants, issue invoices, verify payments, deliver purchased codes, manage stores and payouts, investigate errors or fraud, resolve disputes and maintain appropriate records. Processing must have a lawful basis under applicable Ghanaian law, which may include your consent, steps needed for a requested service or contract, a legal obligation, or another permitted ground. Merely reading this notice is not blanket consent to every possible use.

Required fields support the selected process. If you do not provide necessary information, we may be unable to proceed; ask support whether an alternative is available. Optional information should remain optional. A materially different purpose should be explained before information is used for it. Service messages are distinct from promotional marketing; contact us to object to marketing or withdraw any marketing consent.

6. Who may receive information

Administrators handling your request can review submitted answers, messages and payment evidence. Admins can export application details to Excel or PDF for service administration. These exports contain personal information and must be handled as private records. Store operators may see order, beneficiary and financial details associated with their store; this does not give them unrestricted access to all customer applications.

Relevant information may be shared with payment processors, hosting and database providers, image-delivery providers, communications providers and fulfilment partners. For example, data-bundle fulfilment uses an external provider API; payment processing uses Paystack; uploaded public branding images can be stored through Cloudflare Images. A requested document or placement service may require information to be presented to the relevant institution, authority or authorised professional. Ask which recipient is involved before submitting highly sensitive material if this has not been explained.

Disclosure may also be necessary to respond to a lawful request, investigate misuse or protect legal rights. A change in business ownership may involve transferring relevant records subject to applicable protections and notice. This notice does not authorise the sale of personal information or unrelated disclosure.

7. Public images, WhatsApp and external services

Store logos, cover images and published contact details are intended to be visible publicly. Do not upload Ghana Cards, passport pages, signatures or private receipts through a public store-image uploader. Use the designated private proof-upload or application channel where provided, or ask support for an appropriate submission method.

WhatsApp links leave this website and open a separate service with its own privacy terms. Messages sent there become part of that conversation. A community group is not a private application channel: other members may see your number and what you post. Never post identity records, payment proofs or confidential application details in a community group. External images and embedded resources may also receive technical connection information when loaded.

8. Storage location and safeguards

External hosting, payment and communications services may process information outside Ghana. The applicable recipient, purpose and safeguards should be assessed before sensitive information is transferred. Ask us for information about arrangements relevant to your application.

The app uses account authentication, role-based checks for application records, hashed account passwords and payment verification controls. These measures do not make every channel risk-free or guarantee absolute security. Protect your credentials, verify payment instructions and report suspected unauthorised access promptly. Any security incident will be assessed and handled in accordance with applicable notification and response obligations.

9. Retention and deletion

Different records serve different purposes. Applications and correspondence may be needed while a service is active and for follow-up, corrections or disputes. Payment and accounting records may need to remain available to meet legal or recordkeeping obligations. Security logs, backups and downloaded exports may follow separate retention processes. Records should not be kept longer than justified by their purpose and applicable obligations.

The app does not currently promise a fixed automatic deletion period or a self-service delete-all function. Request a retention explanation or deletion through support. We will assess the request, identify any records that must be retained and explain relevant restrictions. Deletion from active systems does not necessarily remove every backup immediately or delete copies held independently by authorities or other recipients.

10. Your rights and privacy requests

Subject to Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate details, and deletion where appropriate. You may object to processing or withdraw consent where consent is the basis, subject to applicable exceptions. Withdrawal does not undo lawful processing already completed and may affect whether a requested service can continue.

Contact us with your account email, application reference and the request you wish to make. We may need proportionate identity verification to avoid disclosing records to the wrong person; do not send a full identity document unless we explain why it is needed and how to provide it safely. You may raise a complaint with Ghana's Data Protection Commission. This notice does not remove your statutory rights.

11. Changes and contact

We will update the date when this notice changes. Significant changes affecting an active service should be brought to your attention through an appropriate service or account notice. A new notice does not retrospectively authorise unrelated processing.
